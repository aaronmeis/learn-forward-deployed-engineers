The idea

Why Forward Deployed Engineering Exists

Enterprise AI revenue is bottlenecked by production deployment, not model demos. FDEs bridge the gap between complex platforms and non-technical buyers who need outcomes, not tools.

🎯 Outcome, Not Product

The customer buys a business result — higher throughput, better decisions — not a software license or an engineer's time. FDEs own delivery end-to-end.

🏗️ Platform Primitives

FDEs assemble solutions from shared building blocks (~60% reused, ~40% custom). Without a platform, you're a dev shop with 55 unmaintainable repos.

🔄 Productization Loop

Every field engagement scouts for generalizable patterns. Custom work feeds back into the platform, reducing future deployment cost and building a durable moat.

🤖 AI Amplification

AI FDE agents automate ontology building, data transforms, and migrations — letting human FDEs focus on judgment-intensive customer problems.
